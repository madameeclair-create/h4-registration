Supplementary Data 1. Numerical tables and source data.

Table_*.csv contain the values displayed in Tables 1–6 of the manuscript, including published baselines as identified in the table notes. They are not newly generated experimental results. Figure_2_source_data.csv contains the frozen full-precision records used to plot Figure 2. main_results_full_precision.csv gives our six benchmark/descriptor main results with success counts. Rotation and translation errors are averaged over successful registrations only. Timing tables are in milliseconds and contain stage means. These files do not redistribute benchmark scans, descriptor correspondences, or per-case pose archives.
